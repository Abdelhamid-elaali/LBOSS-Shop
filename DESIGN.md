## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-octane, luxury-tech retail identity tailored for the next generation of power consumers. The experience balances unyielding confidence with razor-sharp editorial restraint—merging haute horlogerie prestige with the neon-infused precision of high-end consumer technology. 

Visual style highlights:
- **Dark Luxury Glassmorphism:** Deep chromatic ink surfaces paired with frosted optic panels, luminous ambient backlights, and ultra-crisp micron borders.
- **Dynamic Energy:** High-voltage accent transitions through a 135° dual-energy gradient running from radiant gold to electric violet, balanced by expansive dark negative space.
- **Boss-Level Poise:** Geometric, structured layouts with surgical typographic hierarchies, tabular precision in financial data, and tactile interaction surfaces.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for a dark-first luxury digital storefront, ensuring WCAG AA compliance across text layers and primary conversion states.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0D12`):** Pure Ink Black. Absorbs light and provides infinite depth for product staging.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#14171F`):** Deep Obsidian. Used for default containers, cards, and bottom sheets.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1C2029`):** Elevated Obsidian. Applied to modals, flyouts, tooltips, and elevated card tiers.
- **Primary Accent (`#F5B700` - Boss Gold):** Hyper-saturated golden radiance. Reserved for high-value actions, conversion triggers, active indicators, and critical metrics.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7C5CFF` - Electric Violet):** Cybernetic violet for focus states, futuristic micro-accents, special edition tags, and dual-tone gradient anchors.
- **Signature Gradient:** Linear 135° angle starting at `#F5B700` and ending at `#7C5CFF`, applied to hero typography, VIP badges, and premium action states.
- **Semantic Suite:**
  - Success: `#22C55E`
  - Warning: `#F59E0B`
  - Error: `#EF4444`
  - Info: `#38BDF8`
- **Neutral Scale:**
  - White / Gray 50: `#F8FAFC` (Headline copy, max emphasis)
  - Gray 200: `#E2E8F0` (Body text, medium contrast)
  - Gray 400: `#94A3B8` (Muted labels, secondary specifications)
  - Gray 700: `#334155` (Subtle dividers, inactive elements)
  - Gray 900: `#0F172A` (Background sub-tier)

## Typography

The typographical structure blends display architectural weight with utilitarian clarity and fiscal telemetry:
- **Headlines (Sora):** Structural, geometric, and authoritative. Tightened with negative tracking (`-1%` to `-2%`) to create solid, monolithic brand expressions.
- **Body & Editorial (Manrope):** Ergonomic sans-serif providing optimal readability on illuminated OLED screens. Weights stay locked between `400` (Regular) and `600` (SemiBold) to ensure high legibility at all scale points.
- **Telemetry & Numbers (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for prices, SKUs, inventory gauges, technical product specifications, and checkout totals. Enforces strict tabular numerals (`tnum`) for effortless comparative scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8px harmonic spacing engine governs all spatial decisions.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1440px`, with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 64px (`4rem`) outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, 24px gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) canvas margins.

### Spacing Tokens
- `space-xs` (8px): Icon-to-text spacing, atomic tag padding.
- `space-sm` (16px): Standard form element gap, compact card padding.
- `space-md` (24px): Standard card interior padding, dense grid rows.
- `space-lg` (32px): Primary section stack, module separations.
- `space-xl` (48px): Major block breaks and category divisions.
- `space-2xl` (64px): Hero canvas margins and macro section offsets.
- `space-3xl` (96px): Cinematic marquee spacing and editorial breathers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is rendered through luminous physics rather than legacy drop shadows. High-gloss elements emerge naturally from deep space via optic glass layering.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Foundation (`#0B0D12`):** Total ground plane zero.
2. **Standard Surface (`#14171F`):** Standard container plane.
3. **Elevated Surface (`#1C2029`):** Interactive cards, hover targets, dynamic panels.

### Glassmorphism & Light Emittance
- **Optic Frosted Glass:** Background fill set to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` combined with a 20px blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Micron Borders:** Elements employ a 1px boundary stroke at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. For active states or featured cards, the stroke transitions to a subtle linear gold/violet fade.
- **Ambient Glows:** Floating surfaces (modals, VIP popovers, primary cart triggers) project dual-colored ambient halos:
  - Default Elevated: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
  - Active Glow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(245, 183, 0, 0.25)`
  - Cyber Focus Glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.4)`

## Shapes

The design uses an intentional, multi-tier radius hierarchy:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons):** 12px border radius provides a sculpted, tactile interaction target.
- **Enclosures (Cards, Modal Drawers, Panels):** 16px radius creates modern, architectural frames around product visuals.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** 999px fully rounded geometry for tags, status indicators, counter badges, and compact filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Boss Button:** Boss Gold `#F5B700` background with deep obsidian `#0B0D12` bold typography (`Sora`, 600 weight). 12px radius. On hover: slight y-axis translation (-2px) accompanied by a 16px gold glow.
- **Secondary (Cyber Glass):** Frosted glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover: border tints to Electric Violet `#7C5CFF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No surface, text in `#E2E8F0` with underline hover in Boss Gold.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Card Chassis:** 16px border-radius, Surface Level 1 background (`#14171F`) or Frosted Glass. 1px outer stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover lifts card by 4px, deepening the drop shadow and increasing border brilliance to `rgba(245, 183, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Image Scaffolding:** Subtle radial vignette background to highlight 3D luxury renders.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** 12px radius, `#14171F` background, 1px border in `#334155`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Input text in `#F8FAFC` (`Manrope`), placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px Electric Violet `#7C5CFF` border with a 3px outer glow ring at `rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.25)`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Pills:** 999px radius, `JetBrains Mono` at 12px font size.
- **VIP / Exclusive Edition Badge:** 135° Boss Gold to Electric Violet gradient background, text in `#0B0D12` bold.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Frosted backdrop, `#94A3B8` text; transitions to solid `#1C2029` with Boss Gold border when selected.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Element:** 20px dimension with 4px radius (checkbox) or circular (radio). Border in `#334155`.
- **Selected State:** Filled with `#F5B700`, checkmark glyph in `#0B0D12`.

### Telemetry Price Tickers & Stock Meters
- **Price Block:** Monospaced tabular figures (`JetBrains Mono`) with oversized currency indicators in Boss Gold.
- **Scarcity / Stock Meter:** 4px linear progress strip. Empty track in `#1C2029`, filled indicator utilizing the signature gold-violet gradient for limited editions, or `#EF4444` when inventory drops below critical thresholds.
